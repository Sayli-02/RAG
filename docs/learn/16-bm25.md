# BM25

1. **One-line definition**
BM25 gives a chunk a keyword score: rare words count more than words that appear in almost every chunk, and repeating a word helps only up to a point.

2. **Why it exists** (the problem it solves)
Counting raw word overlap treats "the" the same as "NBFC." A useful keyword score pays attention to how unusual the word is in this library.

3. **Analogy**
It is like a librarian who gets more interested when you name a rare term, and who does not get ten times more interested just because you said that term ten times.

4. **How it works** (step by step)
- **Step 1:** The question and each chunk are split into lowercase words.
- **Step 2:** BM25 scores every chunk. A word that appears in few chunks adds more than a word that appears in many.
- **Step 3:** Scores of zero are dropped, so a question with no shared words returns no keyword hits.
- **Step 4:** The top 10 scores are passed to hybrid fusion.

6. **Common mistakes**
- Feeding BM25 an empty library. `bm25_rank` returns an empty list instead.
- Leaving punctuation attached to words. `tokenize` keeps letters and digits only.
- Assuming BM25 understands synonyms. It does not. That is what the embedding search is for.
